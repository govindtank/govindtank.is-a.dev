OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Orbital Breaker: Kinetic Rogue

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Developer: Govind Tank

Thank you for playing Orbital Breaker: Kinetic Rogue. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent disclosures regarding data usage.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Orbital Breaker does not collect, record, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, location, or biometric data).

2. Local Device Storage

High scores, sector records, Stellar Shards currency, unlocked skins, sound volume, and haptic vibration preferences are stored exclusively on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences.

3. Advertisements & Google AdMob

Orbital Breaker is monetized using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID) and performance metrics to serve non-invasive advertisements, avoid fraud, and enforce frequency caps.

Learn more at Google's Partner Privacy Notice.
