OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Gravity Grid: Neon Shatter

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Developer: Govind Tank

Thank you for playing Gravity Grid: Neon Shatter. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent disclosures regarding data usage.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Gravity Grid does not collect, record, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, location, or biometric data).

2. Local Device Storage

High scores, total shards shattered, longest combo streaks, sound volume, and haptic vibration preferences are stored exclusively on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences. This data is deleted if the app is uninstalled or cleared.

3. Advertisements & Google AdMob

Gravity Grid is monetized using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID) and performance metrics to serve non-invasive advertisements, avoid fraud, and enforce frequency caps.

Learn more about how Google handles ad data at Google's Partner Privacy Notice.

4. Children's Privacy

Gravity Grid is designed for all audiences (rated Everyone) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback, please reach out directly at: govindtank600@gmail.com.
